Why Vanguard

Your data stays with you. Every tool on this site runs inside your browser. Photos, links and passwords are never uploaded, because there is no server to send them to.

Learning without the legal risk. Testing attacks on systems you do not own is a crime under Pakistan's PECA 2016. Our lab gives you a safe target built for practice.

Security sized for small teams. Local businesses need clear steps they can act on this week, so we focus on phishing, passwords and everyday habits.
